# Privacy overview

TWYS is a Windows desktop application, not a hosted broker account.

- Trade journals, recorded emotions, screenshot caches, local watchlists and app settings are stored on your Windows profile.
- The v1.0 first launch creates a new local profile; older TWYS profiles remain on the device until **you** remove them.
- Connecting to Notion or an AI provider is optional. When connected, TWYS must communicate with the chosen provider to perform requested operations.
- Public market charts, social searches and economic releases are retrieved from their respective external services, subject to provider privacy policies.
- The application source and installer contain no user journal, account tokens, or personal records.
- Disconnecting Notion does not by itself delete trades already cached locally. Use the local profile tools to start clean.

This notice describes the intended application behavior. Public-source review and individual provider policies remain important.
