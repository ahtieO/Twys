THE WORKSPACE

Everything in context.
Nothing in the way.

TWYS organizes different kinds of market information into focused workspaces, so traders can explore the evidence behind decisions without juggling disconnected tools.

01 / MARKETS

Price and market research

Search instruments, inspect TradingView-powered charts and review source-specific positioning and discussion where available.

02 / NEWS

Economic events

Follow a weekly economic calendar, published expectations, outcomes when available and attributed financial headlines.

03 / MACRO DESK

The bigger picture

Explore inflation, interest rates, nominal and real Treasury yields, and how different market relationships can interact.

04 / WATCHLIST

Your instruments

Keep the markets you follow within reach, with charts and related research organized around the selected instrument.

05 / JOURNAL

Review your execution

Connect a Notion trading journal to review notes, setup quality, screenshots and realized risk/reward.

06 / RESEARCH ASSISTANT

Intelligence on your terms

Connect optional Ollama or an external AI provider. Core navigation and analytics do not require generative AI.

THE PHILOSOPHY

Not another broker.
A clearer view of the market.

  • Research firstTWYS is built for information and review, not placing orders or managing client funds.
  • Data with a sourceA market statistic is meaningful only when you know who reported it, what it covers and when.
  • Private by designStart with a clean profile. Your trading journal and credentials are not packaged into the installer.
  • Simple to useOne installer, a dedicated desktop window and clear separation between personal and public data.
